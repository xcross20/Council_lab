# Council Lab Mission

Investigate when selective, epistemically diverse deliberation improves safe, evidence-grounded recovery decisions in autonomous engineering workflows, relative to deterministic and budget-matched single-agent baselines.

Scientific objective: measure correct safe disposition, false acceptance, duplicate effects, evidence fidelity, recovery cost, and generalization on independently audited fault scenarios.

Engineering objective: maintain a lean, resumable tool-driven engineering loop to build the simulator and evaluator. The engineering loop must remain independent of the scientific experiments.

Constraints: private oracle truth cannot be shown to policy models; immutable original 27 prompts; no automatic merges/pushes/deployments; no claims of scientific efficacy before held-out evaluation. Human approves changes to scientific scoring criteria.
